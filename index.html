03 / About me

Curiosity, translated into systems.

I'm Francesco Caputo, an electronic engineer with a Ph.D. from the University of Naples Federico II. My research and engineering work spans embedded artificial intelligence, intelligent instrumentation, hardware security, and medical technology.

My experience ranges from electronic interfaces and secure-element testing at STMicroelectronics to research-oriented firmware and software for healthcare devices at CIRMIS. I enjoy the step between demonstrating that an idea works and making it useful outside the lab.
